The Best Movies According to Andrey

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Idea from: Roger Ebert Movie Reviews site

"Good Will Hunting" (1997)

Genre: Coming-of-Age / Psychological Drama / Drama / Romance

"Good Will Hunting" is a timeless coming-of-age film about facing your demons and accepting love and growth. It's emotionally intelligent, sincere without being saccharine, and packed with unforgettable moments. It endures not because of its plot, but because of its honesty-and its profound empathy for damaged people trying to find their way.

"The Lives of Others" (2006)

Genre: Political Thriller / Psychological Drama / Spy / Drama / Mystery / Thriller

"The Lives of Others" is not just one of the best German films of the 21st century-it's one of the best films of the 21st century. A powerful reminder that even in the darkest systems, moments of humanity can shine through. Thought-provoking, heartbreaking, and ultimately hopeful.

"Hell or High Water" (2016)

Genre: Contemporary Western / Heist / Police Procedural / Tragedy / Crime / Drama / Thriller / Western

"Hell or High Water" is a taut, intelligent thriller wrapped in a modern Western shell. It's equal parts heist film and elegy for the American Dream, with enough heart and grit to stick with you long after the credits roll. A must-watch for fans of slow-burn crime dramas like No Country for Old Men or Sicario.
